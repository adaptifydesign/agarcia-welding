About A.Garcia Welding

We pride ourselves in offering our customers unique wrought iron designs. Each project contains a creative approach in style and elegance. We currently serve the Inland Empire — Murrieta, Temecula, Lake Elsinore, Perris, Fallbrook and Corona. Please take a look at our gallery or contact us for more information.

With 12+ years of experience, A.Garcia Welding provides customers with a variety of modern and traditional styles. Every gate, fence and railing is measured, fabricated and finished for the property it belongs to. We consider each piece a form of art and a representation of the client's home.
